# Financial news profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding money, markets, companies, economic policy, or major political events that affect them, with a United States focus.

Typical items include US macroeconomic data (CPI, PCE, payrolls, GDP, retail sales), Federal Reserve decisions and speeches, Treasury yields and credit conditions, the S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow and sector moves, company earnings and guidance, mergers and acquisitions, IPOs and financing, valuations, tariffs and trade policy, fiscal and regulatory policy, elections, government shutdowns, and wars or geopolitical events with clear economic consequences.

Route earnings, financing, valuation, acquisitions, and material business changes at technology companies here. Route product releases, research results, software updates, security incidents, and engineering developments to the technology news profile.

Do not route routine daily price movements, unsupported market rumors, promotional investment content, or generic personal-finance advice here. Include major political events even when markets have not reacted yet, as long as the economic or policy consequence is material.
